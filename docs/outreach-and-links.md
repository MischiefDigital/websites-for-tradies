# Outreach and link building

Audit section 08 and roadmap item 22. Authority is the missing third of the
ranking equation — content earns it slowly, outreach accelerates it.

**The rule:** one relevant New Zealand link beats ten spam directories. Don't
buy links, don't do reciprocal link schemes, don't pay for guest posts on sites
nobody reads. All three are detectable and all three are worse than doing
nothing.

---

## The built-in link engine nobody else has

Every WFT build carries a **"Website by WFT"** credit in the footer, linking
back here. That's already in `/terms` section 8, and clients can opt out.

Ten founding builds = **ten relevant backlinks from real New Zealand trade

This is the highest-value link source WFT has and it requires no outreach at
all. Just make sure the credit actually ships on every build.

## The PR angle

**"The only New Zealand web agency that guarantees job enquiries."**

That's a story. Most agency PR is not.

**Don't pitch it yet.** Pitch it once 3–4 case studies exist with real numbers,
because the first question any journalist asks is "has it worked?" and "not
yet" ends the conversation permanently. You get one shot at each outlet.

**Who to pitch when ready:** NZ Herald business section, Stuff business,
BusinessDesk, The Spinoff (business/small business), Idealog, regional papers
in Wellington and wherever the case studies are.

### Pitch template

```
Subject: NZ web company guarantees job enquiries — or rebuilds free

Hi [NAME],

Every web agency in New Zealand promises results. None of them put anything behind it.

WFT builds websites for tradies for a flat NZ$1,997 and guarantees at least one genuine job enquiry within 90 days — or we rebuild the site free and keep working until there is one. As far as we can tell, no other NZ web company offers anything like it.

[X] tradies have taken it up so far. [SPECIFIC RESULT — e.g. "A sparkie in Tauranga went from zero online enquiries to 14 in his first 90 days."]

Happy to share the numbers, put you in touch with clients directly, or talk about why the industry avoids guarantees.

Blair Wilson
WFT — Websites for Tradies
[PHONE] · info@websitesfortradies.co.nz
```

Keep it short. Lead with the thing that's unusual. Offer client access — that's
what separates a pitch from a press release.

## What to expect

Honest calibration so nobody's disappointed:

- **Most outreach gets no reply.** A 10–20% response rate is normal and fine.
- **Links take months.** An association agreeing in March might list you in June.
- **Client footer links are the reliable ones.** Everything else is a bonus.
- **The audit's 90-day target is 10+ referring domains** — mostly citations and
  client footers. **The 12-month target is 40+.** That gap is what outreach and
  case studies close.

---

## Do not

- Buy links, or pay for "guest post placements" on content farms
- Use automated outreach tools that blast templates
- Chase high-DA links from irrelevant international sites
- Do reciprocal link schemes with other web agencies

All of these are detectable, and the audit's whole strategy depends on WFT
looking like a legitimate local business rather than someone gaming rankings.
